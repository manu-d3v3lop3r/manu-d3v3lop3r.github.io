## Context

The project was developed to improve an internal process for managing
complaints, suggestions and compliments.

The process involved receiving information through a form and subsequently
communicating it to the appropriate people.

## Problem

Before the automation, once the information was received through Microsoft
Forms, the data had to be manually transferred to an Excel file.

An email then had to be sent to the corresponding person or to the
administration.

This process involved manual work and could become repetitive.

## Solution

A flow was developed using Microsoft Power Automate to automate part of the
process.

The information received through the form was stored in Microsoft Lists.
Based on certain parameters selected in the list, the automation process was
triggered.

The flow processed the information and sent the corresponding email.

Some messages still required manual modifications before being sent, so the
solution automated an important part of the process without completely
eliminating manual intervention.

## Process flow

The general process was:

1. A person completed the form.
2. The information was registered.
3. The data was processed using Power Automate.
4. The appropriate recipient was determined according to the established
   parameters.
5. The corresponding email was generated and sent.
6. When necessary, manual modifications were made to certain messages.

## My contribution

The development was carried out together with another person.

My contribution focused on learning and configuring Power Automate to adapt
the automation to the actual process.

## Main challenge

The main challenge was learning Power Automate practically from scratch and
understanding how to use its different elements to correctly represent the
existing workflow.

It was necessary to analyse the process, understand its different steps and
translate them into a functional automation.

## Result

The automation was used in a real working environment and helped reduce part
of the manual work associated with managing these requests.

It also made it easier to process the information and generate the
corresponding communications.

No quantitative measurements of time savings are available, so no estimated
figures are included.

## What I would expand today

Today I would expand the automation by exploring more possibilities within
Power Automate to further reduce manual tasks and cover a greater number of
situations within the process.
