## Context

The project was developed to improve an internal process related to the management of complaints, suggestions and compliments.

The process involved receiving information through a form and then communicating it to the appropriate people.

## Problem

Before the automation, once information was received through Microsoft Forms, the data had to be manually transferred to an Excel file and then an email had to be sent to the appropriate person or administration.

This process required manual work and could become repetitive.

## Solution

A Microsoft Power Automate flow was developed to automate part of the process.

The information received through the form was stored in Microsoft Lists and, based on selected parameters, the automation process was triggered.

The flow processed the information and sent the corresponding email.

Some messages still required manual modifications before being sent, so the solution automated an important part of the process but not the entire workflow.

## Process flow

The general process was:

1. A person completed the form.
2. The information was registered.
3. The data was processed through Power Automate.
4. The appropriate recipient was determined according to the configured parameters.
5. The corresponding email was generated and sent.
6. When necessary, certain messages were manually modified.

## My contribution

The project was developed together with another person.

My participation focused on learning and configuring Power Automate to adapt the automation to the real workflow.

## Main challenge

The main challenge was learning Power Automate practically from scratch and understanding how to use its different elements to correctly represent the existing workflow.

It was necessary to analyse the process, understand its different steps and translate them into a functional automation.

## Result

The automation was used in a real working environment and helped reduce part of the manual work associated with managing these requests.

It also facilitated information processing and the generation of the corresponding communications.

No quantitative metrics about time savings are available, so no estimated figures are included.

## What I would expand today

Today, I would explore more possibilities within Power Automate to further reduce manual tasks and cover a wider range of situations within the process.
